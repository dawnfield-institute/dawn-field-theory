# Dawn Field Institute – Commercial Trade Secret Registry (Contractual Template)

This document is a non-exclusive template for participants opting into the optional Registry. It is separate from and does not modify the AGPL-3.0 license. Use requires execution by the participant and the Institute.

1. Parties and Purpose
- Parties: [Participant Legal Name] ("Participant") and The Dawn Field Institute ("Institute").
- Purpose: Permit Participant to maintain time-limited trade secret protections for specific Application Layer implementation details while affirming AGPL-3.0 compliance and supporting defense of the open Theory Layer.

2. No Modification of AGPL
- This Agreement does not alter, limit, or delay any obligations under AGPL-3.0. In case of conflict, AGPL-3.0 prevails.

3. Scope and Separation of Layers
- Theory Layer remains fully open and unenclosable. Participant shall not seek patents or trade secret protection over Theory Layer constructs.
- Registry protections apply only to enumerated Application Layer implementation methods ("Registered Secrets").

4. Confidential Submissions and Hosting
- Participant self-hosts confidential records and exposes a read-only verification API to the Institute.
- Public registry contains only cryptographic hashes and non-sensitive metadata.
- Recommended baseline: AES-256 at rest and in transit; mTLS; audit logs; geo-redundant encrypted backups.

5. Term and Expiry
- Protection Period: 5 years from Effective Date per Registered Secret, renewable by mutual written agreement.
- Expiry: Upon expiry (or forfeiture), Participant discloses Registered Secrets publicly within 90 days; disclosures become public domain to the extent permitted by law.

6. Defense Obligations (Participant)
- Tier 1 (Required): Provide legal counsel participation and submit supporting briefs within 30 days of written notice in matters affecting the Theory Layer or AGPL status.
- Tier 2 (As Directed): Participate in coordinated amicus efforts, provide technical documentation, and (if reasonably required) expert testimony.
- Tier 3 (Emergency): Reasonable expedited support for existential threats to the framework.
- Cost: Participant bears its own legal costs. No fees are owed to the Institute.

7. Compliance and Verification
- Participant maintains the verification API; supports automated checks of cryptographic commitments and compliance metadata.
- Institute access is read-only; all access is logged by Participant.

8. Forfeiture and Suspension (Contractual)
- Triggers: failure to provide counsel within 30 days of notice; non-participation in mandated defense; attempts to enclose Theory Layer; >90 days non-compliance with required reporting.
- Consequences: suspension or forfeiture of Registry protections; 2-year ban for second violation; permanent ban and required disclosure of Registered Secrets for third violation, subject to applicable law.
- AGPL unaffected: Forfeiture does not limit any rights or obligations under AGPL-3.0.

9. Attribution and Transparency
- Participant publicly acknowledges use of Dawn Field Theory during the Protection Period.
- No disclosure of confidential implementation details prior to expiry, except as required by law.

10. Data Protection and Security
- Participant complies with applicable data protection/security laws; Institute has no custody of confidential data.

11. Dispute Resolution
- 60-day negotiation period; optional community mediation; courts with jurisdiction over the allegedly violating party for final resolution.

12. Miscellaneous
- Severability; No waiver; Assignment by Institute to successor permissible; Notices by email and registered mail.
- Entire Agreement with respect to Registry participation; amendments in writing.
