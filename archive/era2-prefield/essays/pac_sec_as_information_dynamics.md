## 1. The Fundamental Claim

**PAC (Potential-Actualization Conservation):**
The assertion that in any hierarchical information structure, the total capacity at one level equals the sum of realized capacities at finer levels:

$$\Psi(k) = \Psi(k+1) + \Psi(k+2)$$

This is not an assumption—it is the statement that **potential IS actualization at finer scales**. What could exist at level k is precisely what does exist when resolved to levels k+1 and k+2.

**SEC (Symbolic Entropy Collapse):**
The dynamics of how distributed information states collapse toward attractors. SEC describes the balance between:
- **Attraction** (pattern formation, coherence, structure)
- **Repulsion** (entropy production, dispersal, randomization)

At equilibrium, these balance in the ratio 4:1 (PAC:SEC), derived from Fibonacci closure.

**MED (Macro Emergence Dynamics):**
The description of how PAC/SEC dynamics produce bounded complexity at macro scales—why turbulence doesn't explode, why neural networks stabilize, why black holes don't grow without limit.
