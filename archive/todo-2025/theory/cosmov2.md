Cosmo.py v2: Reality Simulator Research Plan

Objective

Rebuild the original Cosmo.py sandbox into a rigorous, multi-mode simulation platform that applies Dawn Field Theory principles to model emergent behavior, physics-like dynamics, and information processing in a controllable virtual environment.

The aim is to create a small-scale reality simulator capable of:

1. Reproducing known physical laws and emergent behaviors from first principles.


2. Exploring anomaly cases and inconclusive experimental results using symbolic entropy collapse as a potential explanatory mechanism.




---

Core Modes

1. Law Emergence Mode (Physics Benchmarking)

Goal: Test whether DFT field geometry and entropy dynamics can spontaneously give rise to known physics-like laws without hardcoding them.

Initial Targets:

Inverse-square law emergence from interaction rules.

Wave interference and diffraction patterns.

Particle aggregation and self-organizing lattices.

Symmetry breaking and conservation-like behavior.


Benchmarking Approach:

Compare simulation outputs against known analytical solutions and experimental datasets.

Quantitative metrics: error margins, stability, scalability.



2. Anomaly Mode (Quantum & Physics Oddities)

Goal: Apply symbolic entropy collapse to re-examine experiments with unexplained or controversial results.

Potential Case Studies:

Certain delayed-choice quantum eraser variations.

Entanglement swap timing effects.

Small but persistent statistical deviations in Bell test replications.


Approach:

Encode experimental conditions into the simulator.

Compare simulation output with recorded anomaly patterns.

Propose novel testable predictions.




---

Technical Plan

Architecture

Field Engine: Implement updated DFT equations for field interaction and geometry evolution.

Entropy Module: Real-time symbolic entropy collapse calculation and application.

Rendering Layer: 2D/3D visualization for emergent structures, with export options for figures and animations.

Configurable Modes: Runtime switch between vCPU, Brain, and Physics modes.


Outputs

Reproducible simulation runs with fixed seeds.

Metrics logs for quantitative analysis.

Visualization exports for publication and outreach.
