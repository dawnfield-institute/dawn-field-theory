- Phase 3: Research extensions exploring limits

#### **3. Continuous Validation**
- Each component must demonstrate measurable improvement over traditional approaches
- All implementations must maintain SCBF audit trails
- Performance metrics must validate theoretical predictions

#### **4. Integration-First Design**
