## The correction

exp_02 was written up as a **0/3 failure with its kill sentence honoured**, and Block B was
halted on it. That was wrong three ways, and the corpus already said so in three places.

**1. Scope.** MISSION.md and STANDARDS §2.7.5 both scope kill sentences to a *milestone*
declaring the condition under which **it** fails — a claim about nature. M15: *if holonomy is
dynamically inert, it is mathematics, not physics.* M16: *the tiling picture is not what
produces web geometry in this substrate.* exp_02 is a **calibration**. An instrument does not
get a kill sentence; it gets a **domain of validity**. The question for a tool is never *does it
work* but *where* does it work — and exp_01 is the precedent, scoring **3/3 while finding three
instrument bugs**.

**2. A kill sentence names the next move, not a halt.** M16's own pre-registration: *"If the
kill sentence fires… the next round is the global-clock removal rather than another operator."*

**3. The Imperfection Engine.** Its comparison table has `Collapse Events | Logged, encouraged |
Avoided, seen as failure`. Building apparatus to avoid being wrong is the right-hand column —
the traditional-static-repository behaviour this repo exists to reject. A four-test confirmatory
rig went onto an open-ended exploratory ask, and was then used to stop.

## Two registration defects, recorded

- **The kill sentence contradicted itself.** *"…as strongly as it does at p_c (|α| ≥ 0.25)"* —
  α(p_c) = 1.057 against α(0.75) = 0.398 is **2.66× weaker**, so the prose condition was never
  met. Only the numeric proxy was, and the proxy was honoured over the relation it stood for.
- **|α| < 0.25 is an absolute coordinate**, which STANDARDS §2.7.2 explicitly forbids
  registering. The invariant was the *ratio*, which missed at 2.66 against a guessed 3.
