# prime_gap_ledger — the loop, the gap and the delta (new study; exp_01 sealed and run)

**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Branch:** `study/prime-gap-ledger` (PR #188) · **Seal:** `9213386c` · **Layer:** arithmetic

## What

A new study opened from Peter's reading: a prime gap is a cascade that truncates itself (the sieve by primes ≤ √p
running until it fails to cover the next integer — a local instance with two ends); the global object is the loop of
units mod the primorial P(y) — bounded, boundaryless, in Chinese-remainder coordinates; the primes of a window are the
loop read locally at depth u = log x / log y; **the delta between the local read and the loop, as a function of u, is
the object**, not the gaps. The loop is sampled CRT-uniformly (residues drawn independently per prime — a segment at an
explicit huge offset is *not* the loop) and enumerated whole for k ≤ 9.

## Gates (known answers, before the seal; `results/exp_00_gates_20260907_114513.json`)

Mertens; Buchstab (local/loop density ratio = e^{γ}ω(u), 32 cells; e^{γ}/2 at u = 2); the CRT pair formula behind
Hardy–Littlewood (120 cells exact); the champion gap 6 in every decade; the Lemke Oliver–Soundararajan sign; the
CRT-uniform sampler against the enumerated loop (TV 1e-5); periodic cells. Two corrections before the seal, kept on
the record: a one-unit loop got no wrap gap, and my first draft encoded "gap 4 beats gap 2" as a known answer (they
share the HL weight).

## Registered round: 0/3 by the sealed rules (`journals/2026-09-07_exp01_outcomes.md`)

- **R1 INCONCLUSIVE** — the mean-rescaled shape metric is a bin-edge artifact for lattice-valued gaps (a gap's spike
  hops bins when the sample mean moves by 2 %); where the metric is sound (u ≥ 3) the shapes agree within noise.
- **R2 INCONCLUSIVE** — (a) drift law CONFIRM at all six live depths; (b) monotone decay at the primes CONFIRM
  (0.176 → 0.103 from 10⁵ to 10⁹); (c) meets the loop's value to 1e-4 in every decade but two floors are below 1e-4.
- **R3 INCONCLUSIVE** — ρ ≈ 0.95 from 10⁷ on, converged below its floors; the drift law has no verdict for convergence.

**Recorded, not claimed:** the window's residue bias depends on the depth alone (y = 100: five decades give
0.2217–0.2243 against the loop's 0.2231), and at the primes the loop over-predicts by a constant 0.0088–0.0099 from
10⁶ to 10⁹ — the LO–S bias is the primorial loop's bias at the truncation depth minus a scale-free residual ε(u).
The loop's exact deficits (rationals, k ≤ 9) are on the record. Gap interiors are smoother than random composites.

## Lessons (register)

A drift law needs a CONVERGED verdict; never rescale a lattice variable into fixed bins; sub-1e-4 floors turn "meets"
into a coin flip — register a tolerance; a `str()` on a primorial kills a run (run 1 died at m = 8; fixed; kept).
