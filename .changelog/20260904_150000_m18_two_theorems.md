## Proved (ninth and tenth M18 theorems, indexed in `formal/theorems/`)

- **Forest resultants are perfect squares.** For forests F, G, |Res(charpoly F, charpoly G)| is a
  perfect square when nonzero, because a forest is bipartite and its spectrum is symmetric about 0;
  index quadruples {±λ}×{±μ} contribute (λ²−μ²)². Corollary: Res(q₀, q₁) is a square on every fold
  half, explaining the census multiset {1, 4, 9, 16, 81}. Second corollary: two forests both carrying
  a zero eigenvalue have resultant 0 — precisely the degenerate half the census declares.
  Verified 112/112 adjacency, 120/120 Cartan; **control 35/150 on non-bipartite graphs**.
- **The golden coupling is forced by simple-lacedness.** With defect-edge weight w the construction
  parent's sheet-mixing condition is γ² + wγ − 1 = 0, discriminant w² + 4, so the fold field is
  ℚ(√(w²+4)) — the metallic-mean family. It is ℚ(√5), γ = 1/φ, exactly at w = 1, the simply-laced
  choice. φ is not chosen and not coincidental: it is what a double cover with equal integer bonds
  and one cross-wire must produce. Generalizes the construction theorem (the w = 1 case).
