- Open-source path: Use under AGPL-3.0 only. No extra obligations beyond AGPL.

The registry exists solely for entities that wish to maintain trade secrets in their application implementations while still using Dawn Field Theory.

**Open Source Path (Recommended):**

- Keep your application open source
- No registry participation required
- No additional obligations beyond AGPL-3.0 compliance

- Contribute to ecosystem sustainability
- Still maintain full AGPL-3.0 compliance for Theory Layer
- **No monetary fees - only accountability obligations**

Nothing in this Section alters, reduces, or delays any AGPL-3.0 obligations (including Corresponding Source and network-use requirements). See also the separate contractual template in `TRADE_SECRET_REGISTRY_TEMPLATE.md`.
