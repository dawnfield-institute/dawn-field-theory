## Why this experiment exists

M17's founding observation was retracted on 2026-08-17: the "wall" of percolation 0.007–0.019
was a binning artifact. That retraction examined the percolation route in full. It did **not**
examine the fourth route — *"correlation length pinned at the white-noise floor of 0.63 cells"* —
which was withdrawn by association.

That reading rests on one inference:

> Correlation length **diverges** at criticality, so ξ at the floor is not "no structure", it is
> **maximally sub-critical**.

The inference is only valid if the estimator in hand is one whose value diverges at a critical
point. `structure.correlation_length` measures the **1/e decay of the density-field
autocorrelation**. In percolation the diverging quantity is **connectivity** — the pair-connectedness
function, the probability two sites lie in the *same cluster* — and the density field is i.i.d.
Bernoulli(p) at every p, critical or not.

So the question this experiment answers is not "is the engine critical". It is: **can the
instrument that produced the 0.63 reading distinguish a critical system from a sub-critical one
at all?** 2D site percolation answers it, because the answer is known exactly.

## The two estimators

**A — density autocorrelation.** `structure.correlation_length`, existing, unmodified. The
instrument that produced the 0.63 reading.

**B — connectivity length.** Second-moment correlation length over **finite** clusters, spanning
cluster excluded:

    ξ² = Σ_c 2 R_g,c² s_c²  /  Σ_c s_c²

with R_g,c the radius of gyration of cluster c and s_c its size. To be added to
`core/criticality.py` as the milestone's reusable N-D instrument.

## Tests and thresholds

Grid L ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256}, ≥ 24 seeds per (L, p), p swept 0.40–0.80 with step 0.005 across
[0.50, 0.70] and 0.01 in the wings. Every quantity measured in the same run as its control.

**T1 — Discrimination power** *(postdictive; replication only)*. For each estimator define

    D = |value(p_c) − median(off-critical)| / std(off-critical)

over the off-critical set |p − p_c| > 0.08. Requires **D_B ≥ 5** and **D_A < 1**, and
D_B ≥ 5·D_A. This is the quantity the 0.63 inference silently assumed was large for A.

**T2 — Location without prior knowledge** *(postdictive; replication only)*. B's peak, located
by the same `finite_size_crossing` machinery exp_01 used, lands within **0.02** of the exact
p_c at every L ≥ 64. A's peak does **not** (registered as a fail for A, not a pass).

**T3 — Exponent, and it is the registered invariant. Load-bearing and predictive.**
ν extracted by data collapse: ξ/L against (p − p_c)·L^(1/ν) must collapse across all four L.
Registered as a **relation, not a coordinate**:

- ν lands within **[1.10, 1.60]** bracketing exact 4/3, and
- the deviation runs in the **same direction as exp_01's** — i.e. **ν measured below 4/3** —
  by a comparable **5–20%**.

The direction clause is the real content. exp_01's γ/ν and τ are both ~10% low and the scratch
peak-scaling was 13% low; if ν is also low by a similar margin, the instrument family has one
coherent finite-size bias, and **that** is what licenses Block B to compare a DFT exponent
against this calibration instead of against textbook values. A ν that is high, or low by 40%,
breaks that licence even if it is "close".

**T4 — Non-critical control, same instrument, same run. Load-bearing and predictive.**

Without T4, "B grows with L" is unfalsifiable — *any* cluster statistic grows with L. The
control must therefore be a system where B is measured identically but the answer is known to be
non-divergent.

> **Rejected control, recorded because it was the first design.** Gaussian-smoothed noise
> thresholded to matched occupancy is **not** a null: a thresholded correlated Gaussian field is
> itself a level-set percolation problem with its own critical threshold, so sweeping occupancy
> would cross a genuine transition and the "control" would diverge for real reasons. Caught
> before running; it would have produced a passing T4 that meant nothing.

The control used instead is **percolation at fixed off-critical p, swept in L** — same
generator, same estimator, same code path, where ξ_∞ is finite by construction and B must
therefore be L-independent once L ≫ ξ_∞:

- sub-critical **p = 0.45** and super-critical **p = 0.75**, L ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256}
- requires **|α| < 0.25** in ξ ~ L^α at both, against α ≈ 0.87 at p_c
- requires the ratio **α(p_c) / max(|α(0.45)|, |α(0.75)|) ≥ 3**

This is the test that can kill B, and it is built this way because the habit that survived the
2026-08-17 retraction was *measuring the reference in the same run rather than reasoning about
thresholds.*

## Kill sentence

> **If estimator B's ξ/L curves fail to collapse with ν in [1.10, 1.60], or if B scales with L
> at fixed off-critical p as strongly as it does at p_c (|α| ≥ 0.25), then B does not measure a
> critical correlation length, Block A does not complete, and Block B has no instrument.**

A second, weaker failure counts and is reported: if **A** turns out to discriminate after all
(D_A ≥ 1), then the 0.63 reading did carry information about criticality and the fourth route
of the retracted wall is reinstated as evidence.

## Secondary failure conditions

Any of these voids a positive result:

1. **Occupancy unreported.** Every percolation-family number is reported with its occupancy and
   its particles-per-cell, per the rule the 2026-08-17 retraction earned.
2. **Spanning cluster included.** The second-moment sum is over finite clusters only; including
   the spanning cluster above p_c manufactures divergence and would make T1 pass for the wrong
   reason. Verified by asserting the excluded-cluster count is ≥ 1 for p > 0.62.
3. **Boundary convention drift.** Open boundaries throughout, `periodic=False` passed explicitly
   to estimator A — its default is per-axis Möbius geometry and is wrong on this lattice.

## What this experiment does NOT claim

It does not claim the engine is critical. It does not reinstate *"criticality was never looked
for"* — that was withdrawn on 2026-08-17 on the strength of exp_12's scale-free P(k) ~ k^−1.727,
and nothing here touches it. It does not re-open M16.

Removing a piece of evidence that said "not critical" leaves Q1 **open**, which is a bearing and
not a verdict. Whether a DFT structure-forming system has a critical point is Block B, and Block
B is only licensed if T3 and T4 pass here.
