## The origin, closed into a loop

SEC began (pre-framework) as an attempt to crystallize arithmetic/algebraic structure out
of continuous symbolic fields — prime-modulated collapse, attractors mapped to cycles, an
explicit unification plan targeting the geometry/analysis/arithmetic trinity and Hodge-
theoretic invariants (`hodge_conjecture/unification_plan.md`). The SEC paper later
*withdrew* the Hodge mapping from core claims as speculative — correctly, at the time:
attractor geometry resembling cohomology is not a theorem.

Fourteen milestones later the correspondence returned, not as decoration but as the
organizing structure of the framework's own pass/fail record:

- Everything cohomological (commutators, isomorphisms, orbit decompositions, uniqueness
  theorems) lands at 91–100%.
- Everything demanding a *metric representative* of a class — a position-space
  wavefunction, a composed rapidity between specific vertices, a universal speed limit, a
  positive-definite vertex metric — lands at 25–85%, and each failure decomposes as:
  class content passed, representative demand failed.
- M13.5 *proved* the kernel of the problem: isomorphism-invariant metrics cannot separate
  same-orbit vertices. The metric layer is canonically class-level.
- M14's one great success (the orbit Hilbert space) was precisely the move of working in
  the quotient — in cohomology — instead of demanding representatives.
- confluent_identity (March 2026), built directly on discrete Hodge projection, found
  independently that conservation is within-scope and that pooled (global-frame) claims
  die while per-frame claims survive.
- Midnight's invariant-registration ledger (6/6) found the same covariance principle from
  the observational side.

Three independent arrivals — algebraic milestones, fluid-lattice Hodge theory,
astronomical registration discipline — at one statement.

## The conjecture (stated for falsification)

**Every physically measurable invariant class has an algebraic (PAC/ADE) representative.
Metric representatives of classes are observer-contributed frame data (definitional
parallax, M13); demanding one frame-free is ill-posed, not unsolved.**

Falsifiers (any one suffices):
1. A continuum failure that does not decompose as class-pass + representative-fail.
2. A frame-free derivation of a metric representative from the axioms.
3. Re-posed class-level claims failing where representative-level claims failed.

## Why this is not the 2025 Hodge mapping again

The withdrawn version claimed SEC attractor geometry *maps to* algebraic-geometric
cohomology — an unproven structural analogy about objects. This version is a claim about
the *theory's claim-space*: which statements the axioms can decide (classes) and which
require frame data (representatives). Its evidence is the milestone scorecard itself, and
it is falsifiable by the framework's own next experiments. If exp_01–03 revive the M13/M14
failures at class level, the algebraic-continuum boundary partially dissolves; if they
fail, falsifier 3 fires and the conjecture dies young like its predecessors this week.

## Relation to the open Hodge conjecture proper

No claim is made here about the classical Hodge conjecture. The connection is structural
inheritance: SEC's founding question — when does continuous structure admit algebraic
representation — became, after fourteen milestones, an empirical question about DFT's own
claim-space, with fourteen milestones of data answering it. If the framework's internal
version holds, the early instinct was pointed at the right wall from the wrong side.
