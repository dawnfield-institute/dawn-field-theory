## Abstract

We outline a theoretical program connecting Potential-Actualization Conservation (PAC) and the Landauer cascade mechanism to fundamental features of spacetime: the speed of light, locality, time dilation, and gravitational effects. Preliminary computational experiments suggest that (1) the Lorentz factor is mathematically equivalent to the PAC energy partition between internal cascade and propagation, (2) locality arises from identity conservation in the PAC tree structure, (3) the speed of light is the propagation rate of zero-potential energy on a discrete substrate rather than a fundamental constant, and (4) gravitational time dilation correlates (r = 0.997) with a Layer 1/Layer 2 interaction density model. These results are exploratory and require analytical formalization.

## 1. The Central Claim

The speed of light is not a cause. It is a consequence.

Standard physics treats c as a fundamental constant from which other properties (time dilation, mass-energy equivalence, locality) are derived. We propose the inversion:

1. Energy exists in two states: potential (available for cascade) and actualized (committed to propagation)
2. Internal cascade activity IS experienced time
3. An entity with zero remaining potential has zero cascade activity, zero time, and propagates at the maximum rate allowed by the substrate
4. That maximum rate is what we call c

The speed of light is the default propagation rate of anything with no potential left. Everything else is slower because it's busy cascading.

## 2. The Potential-Actualization Partition

### 2.1 Velocity

An entity with total energy E partitions between:
- E_internal: funds internal Landauer cascade (potential)
- E_propagation: committed to movement (actualized)

The internal cascade rate — experienced time — is proportional to E_internal:

$$\tau/t = E_{internal} / E_{rest} = 1/\gamma = \sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$$

This is mathematically exact. The Lorentz factor is the PAC partition fraction.

### 2.2 Rest

At rest: E_internal = E_total. Maximum cascade budget. Maximum time rate. Pure potential.

### 2.3 Speed of Light

At c: E_internal = 0. Zero cascade budget. Zero time. Pure actualization. The entity has no choice but to propagate at the substrate rate.

### 2.4 E = mc²

Mass is stored potential. The energy equivalent of mass is the total cascade budget. Nuclear reactions release stored potential into the cascade — it propagates at c because it has zero remaining potential.

## 3. The Photon as Minimum Viable Entity

### 3.1 The Landauer Threshold

A degree of freedom (mode) requires kT ln 2 minimum energy to erase/interact with. Below this threshold, the mode is inaccessible.

- E < kT ln 2: Cannot maintain even one mode. Not an entity.
- E = kT ln 2: Exactly one mode. One degree of freedom. One dimension.
- E >> kT ln 2: Many modes. Complex internal structure. Mass.

### 3.2 Properties of the One-Mode Entity

A photon has one mode, which gives it:
- **One dimension:** Moves in a straight line (one mode = one dimension of propagation)
- **Zero time:** One mode can't cascade with itself
- **Maximum speed:** No internal interactions to slow it down
- **One interaction from actualization:** The first thing it encounters, it actualizes

### 3.3 Light as Cascade Exhaust

When structure is destroyed (explosions, decays), potential cascades down to its most actualized form: photons. Light is the exhaust of the Landauer cascade — potential processed down to its penultimate form, one interaction from full actualization.

## 4. Locality from Identity Conservation

### 4.1 PAC Identity

In the PAC framework, f(parent) = Σf(children). A node's identity IS defined by its children's values.

### 4.2 Why Locality

Moving a node to a non-adjacent position in the informational geometry changes its children, which changes its identity. Identity conservation requires adjacency. Locality is not a speed limit — it is the requirement that things remain what they are during propagation.

### 4.3 Geodesics

The photon moves along the geodesic because that's the path of minimum interaction. Any deviation would require interacting, which would mean actualizing, which would destroy its zero-potential identity.

### 4.4 Computational Evidence

In a PAC tree with depth 4 and branching factor 3, teleportation destroys identity 2.6× more than adjacent swaps. The mechanism matches the theory: f(parent) = Σf(children), change the children, change the identity.

## 5. Gravitational Time Dilation

### 5.1 Layer 1 and Layer 2

- **Layer 1 (zero-cost):** Geodesic propagation. No interaction partners. No Landauer events. The structural substrate.
- **Layer 2 (Landauer cost):** Interactions with partners. Each event pays kT ln 2, creates ξ, forwards Θ.

### 5.2 The Mechanism

Near mass: high density of interaction partners. More of an entity's propagation is Layer 2. More cascade budget consumed by environmental interactions. Less budget for internal cascade. Clock slows.

Far from mass: low density. Mostly Layer 1 (free). Full internal cascade budget. Clock runs at maximum rate.

### 5.3 Preliminary Results

Correlation with Schwarzschild time dilation: r = 0.997. The direction and magnitude are correct. The functional form needs analytical derivation.

## 6. Turbulence as the Testbed

### 6.1 Why Turbulence

Turbulence is persistent, structured non-equilibrium. It resists equilibrium explanation. The Kolmogorov cascade is descriptive but doesn't explain WHY it organizes as it does.

### 6.2 The PAC Explanation

The Kolmogorov cascade is a Landauer cascade. At each wavenumber scale:
- Energy resolves into modes (Landauer event)
- Organized fraction stays at this scale (ξ)
- Remainder transfers to next smaller scale (Θ)
- Θ re-injects as potential for the next step

### 6.3 Results

The cascade energy spectrum at 8 modes per scale gives exponent -1.612 (3.3% from Kolmogorov's -5/3). The organized fraction converges to 0.666 (≈ 2/3). The regularity is provable: ξ is bounded across 10 orders of magnitude of injection energy.

### 6.4 The Mode Count Prediction

The exponent is determined by the number of interacting modes per scale. If 3D turbulence has ~8 effective triadic interactions per inertial-range scale, -5/3 is derived from the cascade mechanics.

## 7. Why Recursion Exists

Every Landauer event produces Θ > 0. That Θ is potential for the next interaction. That interaction produces its own Θ. The cascade is thermodynamically compulsory above absolute zero.

Recursion is not a computational trick. It is what thermodynamics does when every event is required to fund the next event. The Landauer guarantee (kT ln 2 minimum cost = minimum re-injection) ensures the cascade never dies.
