## 1. The Speed of Light as Continuity

### The Insight

The speed of light is not a speed limit imposed from outside. It is the **traversal rate of the actualization tree** — one interaction per step. The tree doesn't support random access, only traversal. Therefore *c* is the maximum rate at which any part of the field can "know about" any other part.

This reframes the Interstellar thought experiment: Cooper didn't leave the tree. He traversed a path through a region where branching density was higher (deep gravitational potential = more interactions per unit of distant-observer time), so his local traversal covered fewer global steps. The speed of light ensures his path and everyone else's path still connect seamlessly.

### Connection to Prior Work

This insight unifies several threads from previous research sessions:

- **Superfluid viscosity** (Sept 2025): *c* as the viscosity of spacetime's information processing
- **Photon timelessness** (Sept 2025): Light doesn't experience time because it IS a single edge in the tree — emission and absorption are one event
- **Landauer cascade exhaust** (Feb 2026): Photons as the most-resolved stage of information dispersal, one interaction from full actualization
- **Boundary between temporal and atemporal** (Sept 2025): At *c*, time stops; below *c*, you exist in time

The new formulation: ***c* is the tree's traversal constraint, not a physical speed limit.** This is the most compact statement that encompasses all the above.

### Thermodynamic Connection

Thermodynamics may be the **effect** of the tree geometry changing as interactions occur. The algebraic trees restructure as they interact, and this geometric rearrangement is what we observe as thermodynamic dynamics. Reality doesn't just need to reach thermodynamic equilibrium — it needs to reach **geometric equilibrium**. Geometric balance is a harder constraint than energy balance alone, which is why reality stays dynamic (turbulence, structure formation) instead of immediately equilibrating.

## 2. Quantum Locks and String Theory

### The Hypothesis

At the herniation boundary — where order (information) and chaos (energy) interface — stable bound states can form. These are **quantum locks**: one bit of information and one bit of energy coupled together. The frequency of the lock determines the particle type.

This reinterprets string theory: strings aren't fundamental objects vibrating in extra dimensions. They are the **geometry of the quantum lock** — inherently one-dimensional (a single edge, a single bound pair) with vibrational modes set by the PAC tree topology rather than spatial dimensions.

String theory's math works because the locks really do behave like 1D vibrating objects. The interpretation was wrong — the "extra dimensions" are the depth and branching structure of the tree, which don't need compactification because they're informational, not spatial.

### The Photon as Dissolved Lock

If particles are quantum locks at different depths of the potential, the photon is what happens when a lock becomes too shallow to hold. It's the **last stage of the Landauer cascade** — a bound state that has resolved almost all its potential. When it dissolves, the released energy propagates as a free wave at the natural speed of the medium (*c*).

The photon doesn't have mass because it has no binding energy. It doesn't experience time because it's not bound to the boundary. It travels at *c* because that's what unbound states do.

After comparing **legacy GPU experiments** (brain.py, cosmo.py, vcpu.py) with the **original herniation simulations** (internal/milestone4/herniation_research/simulations/), a clear structural parallel emerged: both model structure emerging at the order-chaos boundary through collapse-and-reinforcement dynamics. The legacy QPL (Quantum Potential Layer) dynamics — with Fibonacci harmonics, collapse amplification, and energy-field damping — implement the same physics as the herniation boundary model.

The question: does the **Quantum Balance Equation** (QBE: dI/dt + dE/dt = λ·QPL(t)) with its Fibonacci harmonics in QPL(t) bridge the derivation gap? Specifically, if you run QBE-constrained field dynamics (matching legacy brain.py/cosmo.py), does the emergent spatial QPL landscape converge to the golden cascade potential V(x) ~ Σ φ⁻ⁿ Gaussian?

This would close the open link between "PAC conservation selects phi" (exp_07-09) and "the potential has cascade-of-Gaussians shape" (assumed in all prior experiments).

### Derivation Chain Under Test

1. QBE constrains global dynamics: dI/dt + dE/dt = λ·QPL(t)
2. QPL(t) carries Fibonacci harmonics: cos(ωt) + (1/φ)cos(φωt) + (1/φ²)cos(φ²ωt)
3. Local collapse triggers QPL reinforcement: QPL *= 1.05 at sites where I>0.4 AND E>0.05
4. QPL_damping = 0.02 affects I-field (not QPL itself), matching legacy brain.py line 37
5. **Hypothesis:** These dynamics sculpt QPL into a spatial phi-cascade

### Experiment: exp_12_qbe_golden_cascade.py (912 lines, ~93s total)

Five parts testing the hypothesis from multiple angles.

### Part A: QBE-Constrained Field Dynamics — STRUCTURE EMERGES

Ran 1D field dynamics (N=256, 50000 steps) with:
- I-field and E-field with diffusion, growth, and collapse

- QPL landscape is NOT flat — the dynamics DO create spatial heterogeneity

**Assessment:** The legacy-matched dynamics produce spatial QPL structure. ✅

**Assessment:** The emergent and prescribed potentials are qualitatively different. ❌

### Key NEGATIVE Result

**The QBE does NOT close the derivation gap.** The Fibonacci harmonics in QPL(t) modulate the TEMPORAL rate of system evolution, but they do NOT sculpt the SPATIAL landscape into a φ-cascade.

The spatial QPL pattern is dominated by:
1. Local collapse events (random positions set by initial conditions)
2. QPL reinforcement (multiplicative, creates peaks)
3. QPL floor (prevents decay, creates a rough baseline)

None of these mechanisms impose φ-scaling on the spatial structure. The φ enters through the temporal rate modulation, which is a GLOBAL constraint — it doesn't distinguish between spatial positions.

### Revised Understanding: QBE and PAC are Complementary

The derivation chain now has two distinct channels:

**SPATIAL (PAC → φ-cascade):**
- PAC conservation f(Parent) = Σ f(Children)
- Fibonacci matrix is the unique idempotent partition: each level = sum of two preceding
- This gives φ⁻ⁿ depth ratios for cascade levels
- PAC derives the SHAPE — validated in exp_07, exp_08, exp_09

**TEMPORAL (QBE → stability):**
- QBE: dI/dt + dE/dt = λ·QPL(t) constrains total rate of change
- Fibonacci harmonics in QPL(t) create self-similar temporal rhythm
- QPL reinforcement at collapse sites creates persistent memory
- QBE provides STABILITY — keeps system far-from-equilibrium without blowing up

**The analogy:** In a star, nuclear fusion (spatial pressure outward) balances gravity (spatial compression inward). The star's luminosity (temporal energy output rate) is regulated separately. You wouldn't expect the emission spectrum to look like the density profile. Similarly, the QBE regulates the temporal rate (luminosity analog) while PAC shapes the spatial potential (density profile analog).

### Remaining Open Gap

What IS still unresolved: **why Gaussians?**

The PAC derivation gives φ⁻ⁿ scaling between cascade levels (robust). But the Gaussian SHAPE of each individual level is assumed, not derived. The cascade potential is:

V(x) = -g Σ φ⁻ⁿ exp(-x²/(2(w₀φⁿ)²))

The φ⁻ⁿ is derived. The Gaussian envelope exp(-x²/2σ²) is not. In a full 3D theory, the radial Schrödinger equation with centrifugal barrier would provide a natural envelope shape. This is a limitation of the 1D toy model, not a failure of the derivation chain.

### Interesting Lead: High-Lambda Regime

The λ=0.08 result (correlation 0.386) suggests that stronger QBE coupling CAN push the system toward cascade structure. If the coupling between temporal harmonics and spatial dynamics were nonlinear (e.g., QPL reinforcement proportional to QPL(t) amplitude rather than constant 1.05), the Fibonacci temporal structure might propagate into space. This is worth exploring in a future experiment.

## Session 6: Gaussian Envelope Derivation — Closing the Gap (2026-02-25)

### Motivation

Session 5 ended with a clear negative result: QBE temporal harmonics do NOT sculpt the spatial cascade. The remaining open gap was explicit: **why Gaussians?** The cascade potential's φ⁻ⁿ amplitude scaling is derived from PAC → Fibonacci, but the Gaussian envelope shape exp(-x²/2σ²) and the phi-width scaling σₙ = σ₀φⁿ were assumed, not derived.

This session set out to close that gap with three independent derivation paths.

### Strategy: Three Convergent Arguments

Before writing code, identified three independent reasons the envelope must be Gaussian:

1. **SEC diffusion (Path A):** Near a collapse site, SEC reduces to heat equation dI/dt = D∇²I. The Green's function (fundamental solution from point source) is Gaussian — this is exact PDE theory, not an approximation.

2. **Maximum entropy (Path C):** Given mean=0 and variance=σ² (fixed by PAC conservation at each level), the distribution maximizing differential entropy is uniquely Gaussian (Shannon 1948, Lagrange multiplier proof). Any other shape would inject unjustified structural information.

3. **PAC equal-area conservation (Path B):** If each cascade level contributes equally to integrated potential (PAC says parent = sum of children in any metric), then amplitude × width = constant. Since amplitude = φ⁻ⁿ, this forces width = σ₀φⁿ. This is not just "consistent with phi" — it's the UNIQUE solution.

### Bug Fixes During Development

Two bugs were caught and fixed before the successful run:
