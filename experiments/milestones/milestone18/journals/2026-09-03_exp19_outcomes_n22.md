**Strict trees have appeared only at n ≡ 0 (mod 4).** For construction parents that is the indexed
odd-k theorem (n = 2k with k odd ⇒ a rational core). For the *other* two species — sector-strict trees
and the asymmetric third species, which at n = 20 numbered 9 and 4 — nothing indexed says so, and at
n = 22 they are absent too. So the non-construction species obey the same parity as the
constructions: whatever the third species folds from carries the constraint "k even", exactly as a
one-5 diagram on k nodes does. That is the first structural fact known about it since r18 excluded
two-bond tree diagrams, and it points at a double cover of *something* on 11 nodes being impossible
for the same reason a one-5 diagram's is.

**T5's prediction was wrong for a reason I could have known before sealing.** The odd-k theorem was
already in `formal/theorems/`; it made T1–T4 vacuous at n = 22 by construction, and T5 should have
been sealed conditionally on parity. I noticed the consequence for T6 (declared k = 11 out of scope)
and failed to carry it to the other five tests. **Registration lesson 11:** before sealing, run every
prediction past the theorems already indexed; a theorem can make a test vacuous, and a seal that
does not say so wastes the run's power. The seal caught my blind spot, which is what it is for.
