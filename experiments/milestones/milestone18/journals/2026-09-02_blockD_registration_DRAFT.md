## 3. Frame declaration (per the frame-artifacts standard, §2.7 item 6 / §2.9 on `docs/frame-control-standard`)

- **Sampled:** Cartan operators of the objects above, in the vertex basis in which the corpus built
  them (builder and seed cited per object).
- **Expectation:** exact algebra of the same operator (projectors as polynomials in C). Same scope
  by construction.
- **Invariant:** grade, sector polynomials, tr(RD), leak, {R_vv²} and the matching form are
  invariant under vertex relabeling (R is a polynomial in C; D transforms covariantly). They are
  **not** invariant under channel: Block D reads the Cartan channel (s = 1) only and declares the
  Laplacian channel (s = 0) golden-free for A₄/D₆/E₈ (computed). D is the graph's own degree matrix
  — weighted objects are excluded rather than given an ad-hoc D.
- **Scale:** uniform bond rescaling changes polynomial coordinates, not the class; registered as a
  relation. **Both readings of "the H₃ class"** — exact H₃ polynomial (count in the inventory: 0,
  a known negative) and the √2-scaled class (T2) — are written here so the reader chooses knowingly.
