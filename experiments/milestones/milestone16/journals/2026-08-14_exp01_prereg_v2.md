## The problem, stated precisely

The engine has **exactly two scales: the cell and the box.** Nothing between them exists in its
representation, which is why the measurements look the way they do — coherent power piling into
low-k (the box-scale identity), ξ pinned at the cell scale, and nothing in between for a web to
be made of.

The reason is that its relation graph is close to complete:

| operator | range |
|---|---|
| Gravity (spectral Poisson) | every cell ← every cell, one tick |
| Normalization | one global sum → identical scalar to every cell |
| TimeEmergence / Temperature / Adaptive | global scalar reductions |
| Confluence | (u,v) ↔ (u+π, 1−v), long-range involution |
| RBF laplacian | nearest-neighbour — the only sparse structured term |

And M13 makes the consequence a theorem rather than an intuition. Identity is the complement:
a vertex's identity is the structure of the graph without it. Remove any vertex from **K_n** and
you get **K_{n−1}** — identical for every vertex, same spectrum, same orbit, Aut(K_n) = S_n.
**A complete graph has exactly one identity.** Feed identity-as-complement all-to-all coupling
and it returns one entity, and one entity clumps.

Every relation in the engine is recomputed, globally and instantaneously, every tick. No
relation is ever privileged by history, so nothing ever becomes anyone's parent — and a
hierarchy is precisely a persistent asymmetric relation.

## The mechanism, from the corpus

Three sources agree, and none of them is a partition.

**`archive/era1-symbolic/legacy/brain.py` — QPL (Quantum Pressure Logic).** Four lines:

```python
val_info -= QPL[x,y,z] * QPL_damping      # the accumulated record SUPPRESSES future drive
if val_info > collapse_threshold and val_energy > energy_threshold:
    QPL[x,y,z] *= 1.05                     # collapse reinforces it, MULTIPLICATIVELY
    QPL[x,y,z] = min(QPL[x,y,z], 2.0)      # capped
    time[x,y,z] += 1.0                     # per-cell clock, counting collapse events
```

A local refractory with persistent history. A cell that has collapsed becomes harder to
collapse, activity moves, regions take turns — `brain.md`: *"QPL regions specialize —
structural learning emerges naturally."* And `time[x,y,z]` is a **per-cell event counter**:
cells that have collapsed often are older than cells that have not. That is a depth coordinate
laid down by history rather than imposed by geometry.

**`pac_knowledge_discovery/exp_05_pac_tree_construction.py` — how levels get assigned.**
*"The hierarchy emerges from convergence structure"*: strong mutual convergence → same level
(siblings); weak convergence → different level. **Levels are inferred from convergence rate,
not partitioned by position.**

**`asymmetric_conservation/core/async_pac.py`** — *"event-indexed execution, not global
timesteps."* Reconciliation fires per node at |Δ| > Ξ. Every node on its own clock.

## What the engine is missing, concretely

1. **No per-cell event counter.** Nothing anywhere counts actualizations per cell.
2. **No accumulating suppression.** `SpinStatisticsOperator` already samples neighbours at
   distances 1, 2, 4, 8 with φ-decay weights 1, 1/φ, 1/φ², 1/φ³ — the *geometry* of a φ-spaced
   hierarchy is implemented and running. But `rho_sim` is recomputed from the current field
   every tick and nothing accumulates. Geometry without history; and history is what makes a
   relation asymmetric.
3. **One clock, set by the worst outlier.** `TimeEmergenceOperator` documents *"Regions of high
   activity experience faster evolution"* and computes `state.disequilibrium.max()` — a single
   scalar over the entire field — assigning one `config.dt` to all 4096 cells. There are no
   regions. Recorded as a defect, not silently reconciled.
4. **Bulk mass generation has no refractory.** `mass_gen_boundary` divides by `(1+M)`;
   `mass_gen_bulk = gamma_local * diseq2` has no M dependence at all, so the bulk channel can
   pump the same cell indefinitely.

## Question

**Does an accumulating local refractory — a suppression field that grows multiplicatively where
actualization fires and subtracts from the drive there — produce web geometry and more than two
scales, where the single global ledger produces only clumping?**

## Tests and thresholds

Grid 128 × 32, 3000 ticks, ≥ 5 seeds. Control = identical pipeline with the refractory disabled.

**T1 — The mechanism is live.** Guards against an inert operator: `ChargeDynamics` is wired into
the canonical pipeline and does nothing, because `FieldState` carries no Q field, and that went
unnoticed. Requires: the suppression field reaches its cap in ≥ 5% of cells, per-cell event
counts have coefficient of variation ≥ 0.5, and disabling the operator changes the run
(bit-identical output fails T1 outright).

**T2 — Geometry. Load-bearing.** All three together:

- **void fraction ≥ 0.30** — control 0.241, exp_09 measured 0.50
- **density CV ≥ 1.0** — control 0.661, *below white noise's 0.741*; exp_09 ~2.0
- **ξ_u > 0.632**, the white-noise floor

The third is not optional and is not exp_09's. At `quantum_pressure_coeff = 0.30` the engine
already satisfies void 0.605 and CV 1.273 while being a **checkerboard** — C(1) = −0.358,
anti-correlated neighbours. exp_09's conditions are purely statistical, with no scale or
connectivity requirement, and a lattice satisfies them trivially; that failure mode cannot arise
in a particle substrate. **T2 passing without ξ above the floor is a fail, not a pass.**

**T3 — Depth.** Levels inferred by convergence clustering (exp_05's method, not a new one)
number ≥ 3, and the count is non-decreasing over the second half of the run. Two levels is the
current attractor and is the null.

**T4 — Does the refractory actually gate?** The per-cell event-count distribution shows a knee
near the saturation count — arithmetically ln 2 / ln Ξ = **12.2** events for growth factor Ξ and
cap 2.0. If counts run far past saturation with no knee, the refractory is being outrun and is
not controlling the dynamics.

> **Postdiction disclosed.** ln 2 / ln Ξ = 12.21 and F₇ = 13, the corpus's gauge-closure depth,
> agree within 6%. That coincidence was noticed by arithmetic while writing this, *not* derived
> beforehand, and it follows from Ξ and the cap alone rather than from any dynamics. It is
> **not** evidence and T4 does not test it. What T4 tests is whether cells reach saturation at
> all. The cap of 2.0 is inherited from `brain.py` and is unjustified; if T4 turns on the cap's
> value, that is a finding about the cap, not about Ξ.

## Kill sentence

> **If an accumulating local refractory leaves the field failing T2 — void below 0.30, CV below
> 1.0, or ξ_u at the white-noise floor — across the registered parameter range, then accumulating
> local asymmetry is not what produces web geometry in this substrate, and the corpus's QPL
> result does not carry from the era-1 collapse field to the v3 engine.**

Passing T1 and T3 while failing T2 **counts as the kill sentence firing.** Differentiation and
depth that do not produce geometry are the same negative result reached more expensively.

## Secondary failure conditions

Any of these voids a positive result:

1. **PAC stops closing.** The refractory must not break `E + I + M` conservation beyond the
   engine's existing 1e-8 gate.
2. **Coherence only in diverging runs.** Any run where M leaves the finite range is excluded and
   reported as excluded.
3. **Geometry only at the grid scale.** Covered by T2's ξ condition, restated because it is the
   failure this engine has already demonstrated once.

## Implementation shape

Recorded now so the thing built is the thing registered.

A `RefractoryOperator` carrying two new persistent fields on `FieldState`:

- `state.n` — per-cell actualization count (`brain.py`'s `time`)
- `state.R` — suppression field, initialised to 1.0, multiplied by **Ξ imported named from
  fracton** at each actualization, capped

subtracting `R * damping` from the actualization drive. Constraints:

- **Ξ named, never a bare literal.** `brain.py`'s 1.05 is most likely a rounded Ξ — v1 of the
  engine carries Ξ = 1.0571 in ~10 places — and Ξ gives a saturation count closer to F₇ than
  1.05 does. Which Ξ (analytic 1.05843 vs discrete 1.05712) is stated in the result.
- Must be declared in `src/v3/engine/pipelines.py` (`CANONICAL` or `UNUSED`) or
  `tests/v3/test_pipeline_completeness.py` fails — it already caught `PACBalanceOperator`.
- Every metric comes from `proof_of_concepts/v4/structure.py` with a passing `selftest()`.
- One rendered frame per condition, including the isolated low-k component and the `R` field
  itself. Rendering has caught three metric faults in this work and no statistic has caught any.

**Local dt is out of scope for exp_01 and registered as a known confound.** Fixing
`TimeEmergence` means making `dt` a tensor, which touches every operator. A shared clock set by
the field's outlier will damp whatever per-cell history the refractory accumulates, so a **null
result here is weaker than a positive one** and does not close the hierarchy question on its own.
Stated in advance so it cannot be reached for afterward.

## What a null result is worth

If the kill sentence fires, the constraint is real: it would mean accumulating local asymmetry
is insufficient without the event-indexed execution `async_pac` uses, and the next round is the
global-clock removal rather than another operator. That is a more expensive experiment, which is
exactly why it should not be run first.
