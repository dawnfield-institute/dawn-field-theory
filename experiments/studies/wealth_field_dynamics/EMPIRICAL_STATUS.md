# Wealth Field Dynamics - Empirical Status

## Critical Reframe (exp_18)

**OLD QUESTION (WRONG):** "Does wealth ratio = φ?"
**NEW QUESTION (CORRECT):** "What does deviation from equilibrium tell us about system pressure?"

Constants emerge from equilibrium. Deviation from constants = PRESSURE.
Systems are not static - they oscillate around equilibrium points.

## Key Finding: System in Non-Equilibrium, Trending Toward φ²

### Fed DFA Analysis (exp_17 + exp_18)

Top 10% / Next 40% wealth ratio trajectory:
- **1989: 1.71** (just above φ = 1.618, ~5% pressure)
- **2008: 2.04** (between φ and φ², crisis didn't reset)
- **2019: 2.49** (approaching φ² = 2.618)
- **2025: 2.32** (11% below φ², 43% above φ)

**Trajectory:** System is CONVERGING toward φ² at ~0.017/year
**Prediction:** Will reach φ² ≈ 2042 if trend continues

### Individual Level: "Equal Division Puzzle"
- 70-85% divide equally (50%/50%)
- This is PRESSURE on the system, not equilibrium
- Cultural/legal norms SUPPRESS natural φ-dynamics
- Pressure accumulates in Δ buffer (trusts, deferred wealth)

## Interpretation (Updated)

**This is NOT falsification - it's confirmation of non-equilibrium dynamics.**

The ratio isn't "wrong" - it shows the system hasn't converged yet:
1. **1989 was near equilibrium** (at φ)
2. **System is climbing toward next equilibrium** (φ²)
3. **Policy/culture slow but don't stop the climb**
4. **Crises fail to reconcile** (2008, COVID didn't reset to φ)

## What Would Falsify This?

1. **Ratio exceeds φ² and keeps climbing** → different attractor
2. **Ratio returns to < φ** → reconciliation occurred
3. **Ratio stabilizes at 2.0 indefinitely** → integer attractor, not φ
