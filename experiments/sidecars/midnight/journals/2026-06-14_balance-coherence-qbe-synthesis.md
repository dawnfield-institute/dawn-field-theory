## 1. Thesis in one paragraph

A connection at full strength is two ledgers in **balanced correspondence**. Coherence `C` is
that balance read as a magnitude: `C=1` is perfect shared balance (symmetry), `C<1` is
imbalance accrued between the two loci. The Quantum Balance Equation governs how each locus
relaxes toward its **own local** balance; that local balancing is precisely what erodes the
**shared** balance that linked the two loci — so **decoherence is the triumph of local balance
over shared balance**. Things are robust because they are momentarily in balance (symmetric),
not by nature — and a configuration balanced at the moment of admission (emission) need not be
balanced at the moment of actualization (absorption). The delta between admission-balance and
actualization-balance is the deformation that proper time deposits — the **memory** — and it is
what light carries to us.

## 3. The synthesis — coherence is balance, the QBE is its dynamics

Three identifications turn the QBE into the dynamics the coherence note lacked.

### 3.1 `C` = shared balance
For two loci `i, j`, let `C(i,j) ∈ [0,1]` measure how much their ledgers are in **balanced
correspondence** — `C=1` when their potential/actual books mirror each other with no net delta.
This is "coherence is the connection magnitude" (the prior note) restated as balance: a
connection at full strength *is* two ledgers in symmetric balance.

### 3.2 The QBE relaxes each locus to its **own** balance
Each locus independently obeys the QBE: its imbalance source `QPL` decays, so it equilibrates
toward its *local* balance over `τ`. This is the same move SEC makes — collapse relative to the
**local** gradient (M13), the locality principle that rescued exp_23.

### 3.3 Decoherence = local balance defeating shared balance (the key step)
Here is the reconciliation of an apparent paradox: the QBE says systems *relax toward* balance,
yet coherence *decays*. Both are true because they are about **different balances**. As `i` and
`j` each pursue their **own local** balance, they drift out of correspondence with **each
other** — the shared/global balance that linked them erodes. So:

> **Decoherence is each ledger equilibrating to its local balance, at the cost of the shared
> balance.** `C` falls not because balance is lost but because balance becomes *local* instead
> of *shared*.

The decay rate is the QBE's `1/τ`; the characteristic coherence frequency is the Ξ-derived
**0.020 Hz**. This gives `C(τ)` a concrete form (exponential envelope at the balance frequency)
— the prior note's missing dynamics.

This is also the deepest statement of the **globalism→locality** lesson that has run through all
of Midnight: the universe's default motion is *toward local balance*, which is why global/
coordinate quantities decohere (die) and local/relational ones persist (survive). Decoherence
*is* localization.

## 5. The admission ↔ actualization asymmetry (the core phenomenon)

PAC's two moments: **admission** (potential declared / emission) and **actualization**
(collapse / absorption / measurement / now). The QBE runs between them. A configuration may be:

- **balanced at admission, imbalanced at actualization** — it has relaxed locally in transit;
  we read a decohered version of a once-coherent emission;
- **imbalanced at admission, balanced at actualization** — it has *found* balance since; we read
  coherence that wasn't there at emission.

So **robustness is not a property of a thing — it is a property of a moment.** A robust
(symmetric) configuration now may have been unstable at admission; an unstable-looking ancient
signal may be balanced now. **The asymmetry `Δ_balance = balance(actualization) −
balance(admission)` is exactly the deformation proper time deposits — the memory** — and it is
`1 − C` read from the receiver's frame (M13 parallax; M15: the observer supplies the frame /
metric representative). Light is archaeology because it freezes the *admission* balance and we
necessarily read it at *actualization*.
