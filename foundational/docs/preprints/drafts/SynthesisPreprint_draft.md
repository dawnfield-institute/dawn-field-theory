## Abstract


This synthesis paper presents the Dawn Field Theory framework, which originated from a speculative concept of cosmic information mining and developed into a systematic investigation across physics, artificial intelligence, and complex systems. The evolution from the original Cosmic Information Mining (CIM) thought experiment to seven specialized preprints is outlined, covering symbolic entropy collapse (SEC), recursive balance fields (RBF), and computational implementations. The framework proposes that information may act as a generative precursor to structure, driving the formation of order through recursive collapse events in dual energy-information fields. Computational studies in quantum correspondence, biological evolution, fluid dynamics, and cognitive architectures provide encouraging evidence for further investigation. All work is conducted under an "imperfection engine" methodology, treating the repository as a dynamic, scrutinizable epistemic platform designed to evolve through community participation. arXiv endorsement and global collaboration are explicitly invited to validate, extend, and refine this open science framework.
