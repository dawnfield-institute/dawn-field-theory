## Rationale

**Question**: Why add these files instead of rewriting papers?

**Answer**:
1. **Papers remain focused** on specific contributions
2. **Mechanistic context is additive** - doesn't dilute core message
3. **Readers can choose depth** - skim paper OR dive into mechanisms
4. **Easier to maintain** - update MECHANISMS.md without touching papers
5. **Preserves original work** - papers stand alone if needed
6. **Better for different audiences** - casual vs deep dive

**Question**: Why include UNIFIED_EVIDENCE.md in EVERY package?

**Answer**:
1. **Self-contained packages** - reader doesn't need to hunt for context
2. **Shows complete picture** - prevents "isolated result" impression
3. **Demonstrates rigor** - 80+ experiments, not cherry-picked
4. **Enables citation** - clear hierarchy of what to cite when
5. **Small overhead** - 24 KB is negligible

**Question**: Is this overkill?

**Answer**: Given that:
- This is independent research outside traditional institutions
- Claims span mathematics, physics, computer science
- Results challenge conventional frameworks (deriving physical constants from information theory)
- Skepticism is warranted and expected

**Extra documentation is essential** - it's better to over-document than under-document when making extraordinary claims.
