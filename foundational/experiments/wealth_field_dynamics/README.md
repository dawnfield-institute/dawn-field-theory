## Critical Framing

**WRONG QUESTION**: "Does wealth ratio = φ?"

**CORRECT QUESTION**: "What does deviation from equilibrium constants tell us about system pressure?"

Constants emerge from equilibrium. When a system is NOT at equilibrium:
- The "constant" marks WHERE equilibrium would be
- The DEVIATION measures pressure/stress in the system
- Systems oscillate around equilibrium, they don't sit at it

## What Would Falsify This?

1. **Ratio exceeds φ² and keeps climbing** → different attractor
2. **Ratio returns to < φ** → full reconciliation occurred
3. **Ratio stabilizes at 2.0 indefinitely** → integer attractor, not φ
4. **Emergence rate has no relation to π/55** → different dynamics
