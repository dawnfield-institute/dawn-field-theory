# Black & White Hole Polarity Collapse Dynamics

## Hypothesis

Collapse patterns in informational fields emerge from the interaction between
entropy concentration, symbolic recursion, and lineage memory, varying
significantly depending on the polarity of symbolic recursion — either
convergent (blackhole-like) or divergent (whitehole-like).

## Status

**Active** — experiment in progress.

## Approach

Simulate recursive symbolic-entropy-lineage collapse under two modes:

- **Blackhole mode** (`core=True`): Symbolic recursion converges inward,
  collapsing symbolic potential into dense focal regions.
- **Whitehole mode** (`core=False`): Symbolic recursion diverges outward,
  with ancestry and symbolic values propagating from inner regions to the
  periphery.

Key metrics tracked: collapse coherence, entropy mean, symbolic recursion
gradients, lineage trace volume, curl magnitude, and Jaccard similarity
between ancestry and symbolic masks.

## Key Results

- Blackhole mode produces centralized, high-density collapse zones with
  entropy approaching 0.99 (near-maximal compression).
- Whitehole mode shows diffusive, radially expansive divergence with entropy
  decaying to ~0.02 (informational dilution).
- Curl remains weak in both modes (~0.0022) — stronger torque injection needed.
- Jaccard index plateaus around 0.3 in both modes.

See [results.md](results.md) for full analysis and figures.
