# Symbolic Fractal Pruning

## Hypothesis
Recursive calculus operations (Laplacian, gradient) can prune a complex symbolic field seeded with fractured, non-uniform geometry, demonstrating symbolic analogs of field collapse, entropy regulation, and symbolic morphogenesis.

## Status
archived

## Key Results
- Entropy initially plateaued due to overly stable or uniform dynamics; after recursive balance integration and drift, entropy began to fluctuate and drop organically
- Active symbol ratio showed coherent decay, confirming effective symbolic pruning
- Visual inspection shows symbolic collapse zones, balance emergence, and drift patterns
- Recursive entropy regulation and symbolic drift were essential to avoid stagnation
- The framework simulates symbolic superfluid collapse and may represent a novel computational geometry primitive for symbolic intelligence
- 256x256 field with 4 symbols, 20 recursion steps, fractured geometry initialization
