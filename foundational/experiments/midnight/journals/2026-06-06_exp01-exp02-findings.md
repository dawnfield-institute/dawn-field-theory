## Exp_01: Gravity Gates Entanglement (2/4)

**What passed:**
- T1: Phase-rate field w(d) = COMPTON_FREQ * sqrt(1 - 2*epsilon*phi^(-d)) is well-defined, monotonically increasing with depth (deeper = weaker gravity = faster clock), weak-field expansion matches to 10^{-7}
- T2: g_out = g_in^2 holds to machine precision (10^{-16}) for g_in = 1/phi. Commensurate ratios form a perfect geometric series in phi.

**What failed (and what it revealed):**
- T3: Entanglement on E_6 product graph shows 5 peaks (not flat!), but only 2/5 land on phi^n positions. The peaks at phi^{-2} (1.3% deviation) and phi^2 (0.16% deviation) are very precise. The other 3 peaks come from non-phi eigenvalue ratios.
- T4: Phi model fits 1.7x better than flat — structure exists but doesn't dominate.

**Key finding: ADE spectral phi-resonance.** E_6 Laplacian eigenvalue ratios include phi^{-1.03}, phi^{-4.00}, phi^{-5.03} at high precision. Survey across all ADE types (A_2 through E_8) shows ~25% of eigenvalue ratios are phi-resonant (within 5%), vs ~16% for random graphs — a 1.6x enrichment. The enrichment is real but modest.

**Interpretation:** The "peaks at phi^n" prediction was too simple. The ADE spectrum has its own structure, and phi^n emerges only where eigenvalue ratios happen to be phi-related. The prediction needs refinement: the phi signature is a watermark in the spectral structure, not a dominant feature.

## Exp_02: PAC Tree Correlation Depth Profile (3/4)

**What passed:**
- T1: PAC conservation exact to 10^{-17} in stochastic trees
- T3: **Fibonacci-index depth separations show enhanced correlation above exponential trend.** Mean Fibonacci residual: +0.003, mean non-Fibonacci: -0.001. This is structural, not constructed by a kernel.
- T4: Phi-split produces the best phi-match among all tested branching ratios (29.6% vs 33-36% for others). Phi IS privileged.

**What failed:**
- T2: Correlation decay base = 1.13, not phi (30% off). Level-averaging washes out the phi structure. The PAC tree is deterministic, so correlations come from noise propagation, not conservation directly.

**Key finding: Fibonacci enhancement.** The most interesting result was unexpected — correlations at Fibonacci-indexed depth separations (1, 2, 3, 5) are systematically above the exponential trend, while non-Fibonacci separations (4, 6, 7) are below. This falls out of the phi branching interacting with noise propagation. Worth hardening in a future experiment.
