## 1. Introduction and Motivation

### 1.1 The AI Paradox

In early 2024, a question about artificial intelligence initiated this research program: *How can Large Language Models generate massive volumes of novel, structured, meaningful information from nothing but electrical energy?*

This suggested a triad analogous to E = mc²:

**E ↔ I ↔ S** (Energy ↔ Information ↔ Structure)

If energy can become information and information can crystallize into structure, then AI systems are not anomalous — they are thermodynamic machines performing the E → I → S conversion at industrial scale. And if this conversion is general, it might operate at every scale of physical reality.

$$\frac{dI}{dt} + \frac{dE}{dt} = \lambda \cdot \text{QPL}(t)$$

The framework generalized into "Infodynamics," describing how information and entropy interact as dual fields:

$$\frac{\partial S}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla I - \beta \nabla H$$

Structure grows where information gradients stabilize against entropy diffusion. Where they rupture, collapse occurs — and collapse is creative, not destructive.

If black holes collapse information inward (Hawking's information paradox), AI systems do the inverse: they emit structured, coherent information from pure energy. AI as an *information white hole* — not metaphor, but thermodynamic dual.

This insight established the fundamental symmetry of the framework: inward collapse and outward emission are the same process viewed from different positions in the actualization cascade.

### 3.1 The Physical Argument for Exactly Two Steps

1. **Θ (thermal residual)** — immediately available at step n+1. Heat propagates at thermal velocity. This is energy.
2. **ξ (correlational structure)** — available at step n+2. Correlations are relational, not energetic. They require one additional interaction to "set" in the environment.

Therefore step n+1 has access to:
- Θ(n) directly (from the preceding event)
- ξ(n-1) from the settled topology (from two events back)

The recursion is:

$$P(n+1) = w_1 \cdot \Theta(n) + w_2 \cdot \xi(n-1)$$

This IS the Fibonacci recurrence: F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2).

In the physical cascade, w₁, w₂ < 1 because Landauer dissipation takes a cut at each step. The cut IS the structure cost.
