# General Experiments Roadmap

Roadmap for exploratory experiments in early or incomplete stages. This provides staging, triage, and integration paths into the main framework.

---

## Scope
- Experimental spikes and prototypes
- Comparative baselines and ablations
- Reproducibility scaffolding for future promotion

---

## Pipeline
1. Staging: define hypothesis, metrics, and minimum artifact set
2. Validation: run replicates, log provenance, generate figures
3. Integration: align with CIP metadata and SCBF/SEC metrics
4. Publication: promote to theory experiments with notebooks

---

## Near-Term Candidates
- Recursive gravity motifs
- Signal processing collapse tests
- Arithmetic invariants and base dynamics

---

## Deliverables
- Triage board (issues) linking artifacts and TODOs
- Minimum dataset and config templates
- Promotion checklist and evidence mapping
