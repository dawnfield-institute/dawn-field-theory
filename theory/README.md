## The lexicon carries lineage

The vocabulary changed three times, and old terms are kept rather than deleted — reading
Era-1 material without the Era-1 dictionary is how a superseded term gets mistaken for a
live one.

| status | count | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `core` | 20 | current, load-bearing |
| `superseded` | 7 | replaced; `superseded_by` names the successor and `why` explains the change |
| `historical` | 23 | belongs to a past era, no direct successor |

By era of introduction: 2 from prehistory, 27 from Era 1, 5 from Era 2, 7 from Era 3, 9
from Era 4. That distribution is the point — most of the vocabulary was coined before PAC
was axiomatised, and most of it has since been replaced.

## corrections.md is a first-class artifact

Not an errata appendix. The framework's claim to be checkable rests on it, and it is the
reason a "universal constant" that turned out to be a local measurement is still findable
in the corpus rather than quietly gone. Entry 1 — `information_amplification`'s 15.56× —
established the discipline everything since has run under.

A correction here layers **forward**. The superseded claim is not deleted, because the
correction is only legible against what it replaced.
