# Dawn Field Theory Preprint Series: Prelude and Guide

## Series Context

This preprint series presents the theoretical, empirical, and architectural foundations of the Dawn Field Theory (DFT) and its applications in adaptive intelligence, epistemic protocols, and agentic systems. Each preprint addresses a distinct pillar of the DFT ecosystem, while together they form a cohesive vision for the future of computation, cognition, and open science.

---

## Series Structure and Thematic Progression

1. **Collapse as Crystallization: Infodynamics, Recursive Balance, and the Dawn Field Theory**  
   *Foundational theory and epistemology; the unifying framework for structure, cognition, and cosmology.*

2. **Symbolic Entropy Collapse (SEC)**  
   *Symbolic collapse, quantum-classical bridge, and the emergence of structure and intelligence.*

3. **AIX**  
   *Symbolic cognition, interpretability, and agentic navigation in post-symbolic systems.*

4. **Cognition Index Protocol (CIP)**  
   *Protocol for epistemic benchmarking, machine-native comprehension, and reproducibility.*

5. **Resonant Symbolic Convergence**  
   *Human-agent symbolic ecology, co-evolution, and resonance in computational systems.*

6. **Recursive Mathematical Plasticity & Entropy-Aware Architecture**  
   *Mathematical and architectural entropy minimization, adaptive design, and robust intelligence.*

---

## Guide to the Series

- **For Physicists & Mathematicians:** Start with InfodynamicsDFT and Recursive Mathematical Plasticity for foundational and mathematical innovations.
- **For AI Researchers & Engineers:** SEC, AIX, and Entropy-Aware Architecture provide new models for intelligence, interpretability, and system design.
- **For Epistemologists & Open Science Advocates:** CIP and the series-wide focus on reproducibility, protocol alignment, and reflexive benchmarking.

Each preprint includes:
- A "Series Context" paragraph in the introduction, situating the work within the series.
- "See also" or "Related Preprints" sections for cross-referencing.
- Standardized appendices: experiment scripts, YAML/JSON templates, protocol compliance notes, glossary, and codebase links.
