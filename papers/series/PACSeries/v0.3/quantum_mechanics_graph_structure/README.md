# PACSeries Paper 11: Quantum Mechanics from Graph Structure

## Overview

This paper derives the mathematical structure of quantum mechanics — Hilbert spaces, the Born rule, the Schrödinger equation, Bell inequality violation, and decoherence — from the automorphism groups of ADE Dynkin diagrams, without postulating any quantum axioms. The central construction is the orbit Hilbert space L²(V/Aut(G)); the Born rule becomes counting, unitary evolution becomes the orbit-Laplacian exponential, and the tensor product becomes the Cartesian product of graphs.

## Honest Failures

- **Graph double-slit (1/4):** orbit interference is algebraic, not positional — no vertex-level fringes. Structural, not a test-design issue (§12.2).
- **Born rule T3 (3/4):** Fibonacci-weighted (non-uniform) states need a PAC correction term (§3, pre-registered).

## Falsifiable Predictions

- Quantum uncertainty requires non-abelian Aut(G): only D₄ among ADE ≤ rank 8.
- CHSH > 2 requires nontrivial Aut(G) on product graphs (testable in trapped-ion / superconducting simulators).
- Decoherence rate Γ² = λ²·Var(H_env), universal across ADE topologies.
